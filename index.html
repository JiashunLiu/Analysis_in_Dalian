Workshop: Analysis of PDEs in Dalian

May 25-29, 2026

Dalian University of Technology, Dalian, China

The Workshop "Analysis of PDEs in Dalian", hosted by Dalian University of Technology, will bring together leading experts and young researchers in the analysis of partial differential equations, with particular focus on fluid mechanics, water-wave problems, kinetic theory, and Hamiltonian PDEs. The workshop aims to foster academic exchange and collaboration between early-career researchers and established scholars, providing a dedicated platform to discuss the latest advances, emerging challenges, and innovative analytical techniques in PDE theory.

Registration

Registration for the workshop is free but required. To complete your registration, please send the following documents to Mr. Jiashun Liu at liujiashun(at)mail.dlut.edu.cn
  • CV
  • motivation letter
  • expected arrival and departure times
  • recommendation letter to be sent directly from the supervisor/mentor
The deadline for registration is March 15th, 2026. All local expenses for early-career participants (PhD students and postdocs), including accommodation and meals, will be covered by Dalian University of Technology. Early registration is strongly recommended. You will receive a confirmation email upon successful registration.

We warmly welcome early-career participants to present posters during the poster session. Those interested are kindly requested to inform us by email at liujiashun(at)mail.dlut.edu.cn before March 15th, 2026
  • title and abstract of the proposed presentation

Participants who are interested in applying for further financial support (besides the accommodation and meals) are recommended to present posters in the poster session; and please kindly inform us by email at liujiashun(at)mail.dlut.edu.cn
  • title and abstract of the proposed presentation
  • requested amount of financial support
The deadline for the application for financial support is January 31st, 2026.
